# Expand requested source-data categories

## Why

The user requested explicit coverage of civil asset forfeiture, killings of
animals such as dogs, and sexual-assault kit tracking and processing.

## What Changes

- Update the standard request, supplemental checklist, and raw request model.
- Extend the existing forfeiture entry and describe animal-killing records and
  sexual-assault kit lifecycle records with their source relationships.
- Regenerate the provider document through its existing generator.

## Impact

Request documentation only. No schema, seed, migration, import-contract, or
generator-logic changes. Preserve the existing no-cost and redaction terms.
