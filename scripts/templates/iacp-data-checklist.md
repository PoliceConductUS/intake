## Data checklist

Please identify which records you maintain and can release. This checklist is
based on [IACP/IJIS RMS Version IV (2025)](https://www.theiacp.org/sites/default/files/2025-05/RMS%20Functional%20Specifications_Version%20IV_FINAL.pdf).
Chapter references let your records or IT team find the relevant guidance.
It describes coverage, not a required file format.

### Categories from the IACP specification

- [ ] Dictionaries, attachments, retention and audit records (§§1–2, 15).
- [ ] People/vehicle/property/place/organization indexes (§3).
- [ ] Dispatch and service-call records (§4).
- [ ] Initial/supplemental incidents; NIBRS submissions (§5).
- [ ] Force, pedestrian/traffic-stop and consent-decree reports (§5).
- [ ] Investigations, assignments, charging and outcomes (§6).
- [ ] Evidence/property custody, disposition and digital records (§7).
- [ ] Warrants and execution/cancellation history (§8).
- [ ] Arrest events, including impaired-driving arrests (§9).
- [ ] Youth contacts, detention and referrals (§10).
- [ ] Officer field encounters (§11).
- [ ] Behavioral-health responses (§12).
- [ ] Existing analyses, maps and dashboards (§13).
- [ ] Existing statistical and operational reports (§14).
- [ ] Geographic reference tables (§15).
- [ ] CAD/jail/justice-system exchange records (§16).
- [ ] Booking intake and release (§17).
- [ ] Crash investigations (§18).
- [ ] Tickets and violation records (§19).
- [ ] Pawnbroker transactions (§20).
- [ ] Civil-document service (§21).
- [ ] Protective/restraining-order records (§22).
- [ ] Permit/license applications and decisions (§23).
- [ ] Equipment inventories, assignments and maintenance (§24).
- [ ] Vehicle acquisition, fuel, repairs and disposal (§25).
- [ ] Staff histories, qualifications, evaluations and schedules (§26).
- [ ] Employee misconduct investigations (§27).
- [ ] Registration records (§28).

### Additional requested detail and related systems

- [ ] Full jail custody history, restrictions, services, grievances and deaths.
- [ ] Prosecutor decisions, court events, sentences and status history.
- [ ] Badge/title/salary history, payroll, timesheets and overtime.
- [ ] Complaints, commendations, discipline, education, FTO and supervisor relationships.
- [ ] Beats, shifts, organizational history and geographic shapes.
- [ ] Task forces, memberships, agreements and deployments.
- [ ] Funding, grants, budgets, expenses, contracts, payments and revenue.
- [ ] All other maintained datasets identified in your complete data catalog.

These details extend the checklist across other systems; availability varies.
The [detailed request model](#raw-source-data-request-model) lists fields and links.
